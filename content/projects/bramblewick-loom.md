The Bramblewick Loom is a bench tool rather than an instrument: it winds small
antenna coils at a controlled tension. The controller counts turns from the
stepper's step signal, so no encoder is needed.

The design package contains the controller firmware, the calibration procedure
and the bench notes from the first winding runs.
