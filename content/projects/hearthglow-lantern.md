The Hearthglow Lantern started as a winter bench experiment: a single cell, one
constant-current driver, and a light guide polished by hand. The prototype keeps
the electronics on a removable rail so the enclosure can be re-printed without
re-soldering.

The design package collects the driver firmware, the enclosure drawings and the
assembly sequence used for the 2026 prototype cohort.
