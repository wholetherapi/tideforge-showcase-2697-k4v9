The Morrowglass Panel began as a replacement for the mechanical switches used on
the collective's bench instruments. The prototype uses a four-layer sense board
behind a glass face and a short firmware helper for baseline tracking.

The panel is documented with a pinout, a demo configuration and the controller
helpers that were shipped with the first cohort.
